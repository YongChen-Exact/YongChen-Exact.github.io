# About me
I am a Ph.D. candidate at West China Hospital of Sichuan University from 2024/09, supervised by [Prof. Kang Li](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Ww6adYYAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN). I received both my Bachelor’s degree (2021/06) and Master’s degree (2024/06) from South-Central Minzu University. My research focuses on medical image analysis, active learning, and domain adaptation. I am particularly interested in developing robust and data-efficient machine learning methods to address challenges such as limited annotations, distribution shifts across institutions, and real-world clinical variability.
