# Biography
I am a PhD candidate at West China Hospital, Sichuan University, under the supervision of [Prof. Kang Li](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Ww6adYYAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN), and a research intern at Ant Group, under the supervision of [Prof. Le Lu](https://www.cs.jhu.edu/~lelu/). I received both my bachelor’s degree in 2021 and my master’s degree in 2024 from South-Central Minzu University. My research interests include medical image analysis, foundation models for image segmentation, and active domain adaptation. In particular, I aim to develop robust and data-efficient machine learning methods that address limited annotations, cross-institutional distribution shifts, and variability in real-world clinical settings.
